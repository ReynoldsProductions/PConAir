Look
Upload logos in admin → Packages → Faire Wire → Upload Image, then pick one here (the list refreshes when you open it). Best results: transparent PNG or SVG, wider than tall, at least 120px tall. It is scaled to fit the bar with no cropping, so square-ish art reads narrow — the bar is only 96px high, so a wide mark carries much better. Use the % to tune without re-exporting.
Typeface applies everywhere in Faire Wire; the accent colour replaces the tan trim on the ticker bug and headline lead-ins, and the lower-third accent bar, on top of whichever theme is picked above. RESET goes back to the default tan.
